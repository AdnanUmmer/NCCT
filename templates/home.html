04 / Understanding architectural lighting

Good Lighting Does
More Than Illuminate.

Light reveals architectural character. Its distribution, colour and relationship to surrounding surfaces shape both the atmosphere and the practical experience of a space.

01

Light Distribution

Place light where it serves the task or surface. A directional spotlight creates emphasis; a recessed fitting can quietly support general illumination.

Direction & focus
02

Colour Temperature

Consider the appearance of light alongside materials and atmosphere. Compare finishes under the intended lighting conditions.

Material & mood
03

Visual Comfort

Balance brightness, contrast and the visibility of the source. Consider the space from the positions of the people who use it.

People & perception
04

Architectural Integration

Coordinate fittings with proportions, details and installation constraints. Linear light can emphasise boundaries without competing with the architecture.

Detail & proportion
